# Locked Startup and Product Principles

1. Intelligence through evidence.
2. Probability over certainty.
3. No-bet / no-signal is a valid output.
4. Market odds are informative inputs and baselines.
5. Every prediction must be reproducible.
6. Every model must be benchmarked.
7. Every feature must prove value out of sample.
8. One result must never trigger an immediate model change.
9. Profitability is measured, never assumed.
10. Complexity must justify itself.
11. Product scope is signal generation, ranking, explanation, monitoring, and paper testing.
12. Automatic betting and real-money execution remain disabled and out of scope.
13. The primary application dashboard remains organized around Reports & Status and Signals.
14. Releases must include dashboard verification, multicore review, and at least ten complete audit passes.
