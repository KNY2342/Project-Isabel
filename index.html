02Locked principles

Discipline is a
product feature.

Project Isabel is designed to say “no” more often than weak systems say “yes.” The architecture protects scientific integrity as the platform grows.

“

The objective is not to produce more predictions. It is to produce fewer, better-defended decisions.

01

Probability over certainty

Every output is a probability distribution with explicit uncertainty—not a promise or guaranteed result.

02

No signal is a valid signal

WATCH and REJECT are first-class decisions. Passing on weak evidence is a successful system outcome.

03

The market is evidence

Odds are treated as informative inputs and reference baselines, not an opponent to dismiss.

04

Reproducible by design

Predictions, features, models, data versions, and decisions are journaled for later reconstruction.

05

Complexity must earn its place

Every feature and model must demonstrate out-of-sample value before it can influence production signals.

06

Measured, never assumed

Calibration, closing-line performance, robustness, and paper-tested outcomes are continuously evaluated.

03Decision architecture

From raw evidence to
qualified signal.

Every candidate moves through a transparent chain of evidence, modeling, challenge, and rejection gates.

01
INGEST

Unify the evidence

Fixtures, teams, players, context, market prices, movement, weather, travel, and live events enter a versioned data layer.

02
ESTIMATE

Build probabilities

Baselines and qualified models estimate outcomes across the complete supported market set.

03
CHALLENGE

Test the conviction

Uncertainty, disagreement, data trust, reliability, correlation, and market information challenge each candidate.

04
DECIDE

Rank or reject

Only candidates passing all gates become ranked signals. Everything else remains WATCH or REJECT.

Product boundary

Intelligence—not automatic execution.

Project Isabel generates, ranks, explains, monitors, and paper-tests research signals. It does not place bets, manage bookmaker accounts, or promise profit. Final decisions remain human.
