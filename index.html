THE PLATFORM

One system, four connected intelligence layers.

Live forecasting, autonomous research, scientific validation, and resilient operations in one coherent platform.

01

Live decision intelligence

Ranked signals with probability, confidence, fair-value context, uncertainty, evidence quality, and clear decision tiers.

  • Real-time streaming
  • Explainable scoring
  • Freshness and conflict checks
02

Autonomous research

A production-isolated laboratory generates and evaluates model candidates without disrupting live prediction workloads.

  • Adaptive model evolution
  • Candidate lineage
  • Multicore research workers
03

Scientific validation

Models are assessed through calibration, walk-forward testing, drift monitoring, stability analysis, and staged promotion gates.

  • Model tournaments
  • Drift intelligence
  • Paper-testing governance
04

Operational resilience

Observability, provider continuity, database health, recovery readiness, and long-run stability monitoring.

  • Provider continuity
  • Production-first scheduling
  • Release readiness controls

ABOUT

Built around disciplined decision support—not promises.

Project Isabel is an independent technology project focused on making sports forecasting more transparent, measurable, and operationally reliable. It helps users evaluate information consistently; it does not guarantee outcomes.

The platform does not place bets or execute bookmaker transactions. It generates, ranks, explains, monitors, and paper-tests analytical signals.
