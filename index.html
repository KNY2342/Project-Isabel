Building the Future of Sports Intelligence

Helping people understand the game with confidence.

Project Isabel is an AI startup focused on making sports analysis easier to understand. Instead of overwhelming users with data, the platform organizes information into transparent insights supported by continuous research and validation.

Our Story

Why Project Isabel?

Because sports decisions shouldn't depend on guesswork alone.

"Understand the reasoning, not just the prediction."

Our Mission

We believe AI should help people understand complex information—not replace their judgment. Project Isabel is being built to organize sports data into insights that are easier to review, compare, and learn from.

The Platform

Sports Signals

Ranked opportunities presented with confidence, supporting context, and clear explanations.

Continuous Research

Ideas are explored in a separate research environment before becoming part of the production platform.

Transparent Validation

Performance is measured through historical evaluation and paper testing so improvements are backed by evidence.

The Journey Ahead

Project Isabel is still growing. Future releases will expand live monitoring, improve the web application, strengthen research workflows, and continue refining the user experience while keeping the platform transparent and approachable.
